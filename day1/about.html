About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a web app on taking notes, fast and simple

How to use QuickNotes

  • Enter you quick note into the input
  • Click Add note
  • Confirm your note appears in yours notes tab

Features

  • One note Entry Input
  • Simpistic design
  • Saves your notes instantly in the your notes tab for review
Shortcut what is does
shift + / Selects the Input field
ctrl + k shows a search bar to search your notes
